# Deck Check (SIH26085)

Round 1 is scored on this deck alone, it carries 20% of the final score, and it is
the only thing anyone sees before December. Check it against the rules and against
the review, then say plainly whether it is submittable.

## Hard format rules — any failure blocks submission

- **Six slides maximum, including the title slide.** The instructions slide from
  the template must be deleted.
- The official template structure must be preserved: Title, Idea, Technical
  Approach, Feasibility and Viability, Impact and Benefits, Research and
  References. Do not restructure it.
- **Export to PDF.** The portal accepts nothing else.
- Problem Statement ID **SIH26085** and the exact official title must appear on
  the title slide, unabbreviated, along with theme (Disaster Management), category
  (Software), Team ID and registered team name. Placeholders in square brackets
  are a failure, not a detail.
- Points, diagrams and infographics — not paragraphs.

## Content checks, each one a pass or fail

1. **Execution timeline present.** Round 1 explicitly scores "execution timeline
   with respect to hackathon duration". The Grand Finale is **36 hours**, not 48.
   A deck with no timeline is leaving marks on the table.
2. **The incumbent is named.** iFLOWS-Mumbai, CFLOWS-Chennai, and ideally IIT
   Delhi's Barapullah system. Any wording implying nothing like this exists in
   India is an automatic fail — MoES built those systems and MoES is the evaluator.
3. **No unsourced numbers.** No accuracy figures, no rupee costs, no percentages,
   no "95%". Unmeasured performance must read as a target.
4. **Resolution honesty.** No claim of 10 m terrain derived by interpolating 30 m
   data. Depth is reported in bands, never as a centimetre point value.
5. **Drainage described accurately** — published GCC alignments with estimated
   hydraulic attributes, never "synthetic" and never implied to be a full
   municipal asset database.
6. **References resolve.** Every link must open. The official problem statement
   link is `sih.gov.in/sih2026PS` — never a third-party mirror. No entry that
   just says "2024 research literature".
7. **Something real is shown.** At least one image of actual output — the drain
   network, a depth map, or the nowcast skill chart — rather than arrows and boxes.
8. **Team composition** is six members with at least one woman, confirmed before
   the SPOC nominates.

## Output

A pass/fail line per check, then one of three verdicts: **submittable**,
**submittable after these edits** with the exact list, or **not submittable yet**
with the reason. Quote the slide text you are objecting to so Bitty can find it.
Say what to change, not just what is wrong.
